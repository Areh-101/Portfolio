Our Beauty Cult

Reimagining beauty discovery through clearer navigation and visual design

01

Overview

Our Beauty Cult is a beauty blog redesign concept focused on helping readers explore products, discover reviews, and browse content through a cohesive visual experience.

02

The problem

Beauty blogs can contain a wide range of reviews, product categories, and editorial content. Without clear organisation, readers may struggle to find relevant information or understand where to explore next.

The design challenge was to make beauty content easier to discover while creating a distinct and consistent visual identity.

Design question: How might we create an engaging beauty website that helps readers move easily between product categories, reviews, and related content?

03

The goal

Create a website concept that helps readers:

  • Browse beauty content through clear categories.
  • Find product reviews and understand their context.
  • Discover related articles and products.
  • Enjoy a consistent experience across the site.
04

My approach

I explored the website’s structure through a sitemap, then developed wireframes to establish content hierarchy and page layouts. Alongside this, I worked on the brand direction to connect the site’s editorial content with a cohesive visual identity.

The focus was on making the content structure understandable before refining the visual details.

05

The proposed experience

Clearer content categories

The navigation gives readers a starting point for exploring beauty topics. Organising content into recognisable categories supports both purposeful browsing and open-ended discovery.

Scannable review previews

Article and review previews establish a consistent hierarchy for imagery, titles, and supporting information. The intention is to help readers assess what interests them before opening a full article.

Connected content

The proposed structure creates opportunities to move between categories, reviews, and related articles, helping readers continue exploring beyond a single page.

Cohesive visual identity

The brand direction brings consistency to typography, imagery, colour, and layout. These elements support the beauty editorial experience while keeping the content readable.

06

Key design decisions

Prioritise navigation and hierarchy.

Readers need to understand the site’s structure and distinguish categories, article titles, and supporting details.

Use imagery with purpose.

Product and editorial imagery should help readers recognise content while supporting a consistent page layout.

Build repeatable layouts.

Consistent content cards and page patterns create a foundation for adding new reviews and articles.

07

Outcome and next steps

The project explored a sitemap, wireframes, product categories, review layouts, and a brand direction for the redesigned website.

The redesign remains a concept. Improvements in content discovery and usability would need to be evaluated through testing.

The next step would be to ask readers to locate a product review, browse a category, and find a related article. Their feedback would help refine category names, navigation, and content hierarchy.

08

Reflection

Our Beauty Cult helped me explore the relationship between branding and content organisation. It reinforced how visual consistency and clear structure can work together to support an engaging editorial experience.
