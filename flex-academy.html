01

Overview

The Flex Academy is a landing page design completed as part of an assessment. The project focused on translating a supplied brief into a cohesive digital experience, with clear content hierarchy and prominent calls to action.

02

The challenge

A landing page needs to communicate its offering quickly and give visitors a clear reason to continue exploring. This project required balancing brand expression, content, and calls to action within a focused page experience.

Design question: How might we introduce The Flex Academy clearly and guide visitors towards the next step across desktop and mobile?

03

The goal

Create a landing page concept that:

  • Communicates the offering through a clear visual hierarchy.
  • Organises content into an understandable sequence.
  • Makes key calls to action easy to find.
  • Adapts the experience to desktop and mobile screens.
04

My approach

I began with low-fidelity wireframes to explore the page structure, content order, and placement of key actions. This helped establish the overall journey before developing the visual design.

I then created high-fidelity desktop and mobile layouts, exploring how The Flex and Base360 colour directions could work together within a consistent interface.

05

Key design decisions

Establish a clear first impression

The opening section prioritises the main message and primary action. Its purpose is to help visitors understand the page and identify where to begin.

Give the content a deliberate sequence

I organised the layout to introduce information progressively. Distinct sections and consistent spacing help visitors scan the page and decide what to explore.

Make calls to action visible

Prominent calls to action create clear points for visitors to continue. Their placement supports the page’s intended journey.

Adapt the layout for mobile

The mobile design reworks the desktop composition into a narrower reading flow, considering content stacking, spacing, and the visibility of key actions.

06

Outcome

I completed low-fidelity and high-fidelity designs, user flows, and desktop and mobile landing page concepts within the assessment’s six-to-eight hour timebox.

The work demonstrates my approach to interpreting a brief, prioritising design decisions, and delivering a cohesive concept under a deadline. The design was submitted for assessment; implementation and conversion results were not available.

07

What I would test next

I would test whether visitors can explain the offering after viewing the opening section, locate the primary action, and understand what happens next. I would also evaluate the mobile reading flow and refine the design based on feedback.

08

Reflection

The Flex Academy helped me practise making focused design decisions within a limited timeframe. It reinforced the value of establishing structure early and treating mobile layouts as a core part of the experience.
