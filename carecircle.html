01

Overview

CareCircle is a mobile app concept designed to help parents discover verified nannies and explore nearby restaurants and activities in Seattle and Bellevue. The idea brings childcare and local discovery into one experience, making it easier to plan a date night or time away.

02

The problem

Planning an evening out can involve several disconnected tasks: finding a nanny, evaluating their suitability, checking availability, and deciding where to go. For parents, uncertainty about childcare can make the whole process feel overwhelming.

Design question: How might we help parents find childcare they feel confident about while making it easier to plan their time out?

03

The goal

Create a welcoming, straightforward experience that helps parents:

  • Discover nannies in their area.
  • Understand caregivers’ experience and verification information.
  • Find nearby restaurants and activities.
  • Move between childcare and outing plans with less effort.
04

My approach

I developed CareCircle around two connected needs: confidence in childcare and convenience in planning. I mapped the parent journey, explored the information needed to assess a nanny, and designed screens that connect caregiver discovery with local recommendations.

The project also considers a separate nanny experience, recognising that caregivers need a clear way to present their skills and experience.

05

The proposed experience

1. Discover nearby nannies

Parents browse caregivers in their area and open profiles to learn more. The design prioritises information that supports an informed decision, including experience, availability, and verification status.

2. Review trust information

Caregiver profiles give verification information a visible place in the interface. The intention is to help parents understand what has been checked and what they still need to discuss with a caregiver. Verification is a proposed product feature; it does not guarantee a caregiver’s suitability.

3. Explore local options

Parents can discover nearby restaurants and activities, connecting the practical task of finding childcare with the experience they want to plan.

4. Plan the next step

The proposed journey connects nanny discovery, profile review, and local exploration so parents can organise their plans within a consistent experience.

06

Visual design

I chose a colourful, mobile-first direction to create a friendly and approachable identity. The supplied screens use coral, cream, green, and supporting accent colours. Clear navigation, readable typography, and consistent interface components help organise information without making the experience feel crowded.

The aim was to balance warmth with clarity, especially on screens where parents assess caregiver information.

07

Key design decisions

Make trust information easy to find.

Parents should be able to locate relevant verification details without searching through a long profile.

Connect childcare with local discovery.

Restaurant and activity recommendations give the app a purpose beyond caregiver browsing and support the wider planning journey.

Design for both parents and nannies.

Considering both sides helps identify what caregivers need to share and what parents need to understand.

08

Outcome and next steps

The project includes user journey exploration, wireframes, interface concepts, a style guide, and a prototype direction for the parent and nanny experiences.

CareCircle remains a design concept. Usability improvements and real-world impact have not yet been validated through testing.

My next step would be to test whether parents can find a suitable caregiver, interpret verification information correctly, and understand how local discovery fits into their planning process.

09

Reflection

CareCircle helped me explore how interface design can support decisions that involve trust. It reinforced the need to communicate clearly, make important information visible, and consider the complete experience surrounding a user’s immediate task.
