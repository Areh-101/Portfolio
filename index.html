02 / ABOUT ME

A creative eye.
A curious mind.

Hi, I’m Arehone Rahulani, a UI/UX and visual designer based in South Africa. I create thoughtful digital experiences that bring together clear functionality, visual storytelling, and attention to detail.

My background in Digital Media Arts and Performing Arts shapes how I approach design with curiosity, creativity, and an interest in how people experience stories, spaces, and technology. I enjoy turning complex ideas into intuitive interfaces, from early research and wireframes to polished designs and interactive prototypes.

Outside of design, you’ll find me exploring photography, sewing, travelling, or experimenting with new creative tools. These interests keep me observant and inspire fresh perspectives in my work.
