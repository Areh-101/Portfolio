01

Overview

Bumbershoot is a group app design project centred on the festival experience. The concept brings schedules, announcements, and a festival map into one mobile interface, helping attendees find useful information while enjoying the event.

02

The problem

Festivals offer plenty to discover, but attendees also need to make practical decisions: which performance to see, where to go next, and whether anything has changed.

When this information is difficult to locate, planning can interrupt the experience.

Design question: How might we help festivalgoers find their next activity and navigate the event through a clear, engaging mobile experience?

03

The goal

Design an app concept that helps attendees:

  • Explore performances and activities.
  • Check the festival schedule.
  • Locate stages and amenities.
  • Find event announcements.
04

My contribution

Working within a group, I contributed to user flows and the design of the announcement board, map, and schedule pages. I also created tile artwork to support the app’s visual identity.

My focus was on connecting expressive festival visuals with practical information that attendees could scan quickly.

05

The design approach

Schedule: make browsing easier

The schedule page organises event information so attendees can explore what is happening and decide what to see next. A clear hierarchy gives performance details room to stand out.

Map: connect discovery with location

The map page supports navigation around the festival. Its purpose is to help attendees connect an event or destination with its location and orient themselves within the venue.

Announcements: give updates a clear home

The announcement board provides a dedicated place for festival updates. Separating announcements from the schedule helps attendees distinguish new information from planned programming.

Tile artwork: express the festival’s energy

The tile artwork brings a playful, creative character to the interface. I aimed to make the visuals engaging while keeping navigation and event information readable.

06

User flows and prototyping

The project explored how attendees could move between discovering an event, reviewing its details, and locating it on the map.

Wireframes helped establish the page structure before developing higher-fidelity screens. Storyboarding and prototype work helped communicate how the app could fit into a festival visit.

07

Key design decisions

Keep essential information easy to scan.

Schedules, locations, and announcements need a clear hierarchy so users can find relevant details quickly.

Connect the schedule and map.

Knowing what is happening and knowing where it is happening are closely related needs.

Balance visual expression with usability.

The festival’s creative character should support a lively experience while allowing practical information to remain clear.

08

Outcome and next steps

The group project developed user flows, wireframes, interface designs, a style guide, and a final storyboard to communicate the proposed experience.

The design remains a concept. Its effect on navigation and planning has not been established through validated usability results.

The next step would be to test whether attendees can find a performance, identify its location, and locate an announcement without assistance. Testing in outdoor conditions would also help assess readability and map clarity.

09

Reflection

Bumbershoot gave me experience designing within a team and considering how separate screens work together as one product. It helped me practise balancing expressive visual design with the practical needs of people using an app during a busy event.
