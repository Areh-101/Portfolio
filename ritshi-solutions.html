01

Overview

Ritshi Solutions is a logo redesign project focused on exploring a refreshed visual identity for an industrial business.

02

The challenge

Explore how the logo could communicate the company’s industrial focus through a clear, recognisable mark.

03

The goal

Develop a logo direction that feels professional, reflects the business, and works across digital and printed materials.

04

Design exploration

I explored logo concepts, considering how typography, shape, and composition could work together. The process focused on balancing a distinctive character with readability and simplicity.

05

Key considerations

  • Typography: Establish a clear and professional company name.
  • Form: Create a recognisable composition.
  • Versatility: Consider legibility at different sizes and on different backgrounds.
  • Relevance: Connect the visual direction to the company’s industrial focus.
06

Outcome

The project produced a logo exploration board presenting proposed directions for Ritshi Solutions. The supplied visuals show the original mark, a redesigned direction, and illustrative applications on a mug, shirt, and pen.

07

Reflection

This project helped me practise translating a business’s character into a focused visual mark and considering how typography and form influence brand perception.
